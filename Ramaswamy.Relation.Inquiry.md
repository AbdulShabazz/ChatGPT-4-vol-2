## Terminology clarification

* **South Indian**: People from the southern Indian states—Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala.
* **West Indian**: Either (a) people from the Caribbean, or (b) people from western India (e.g., Gujarat, Maharashtra). It does **not** refer to South India.

## Important cultural note

Many South Indians:

* **Do not use surnames in the Western sense**
* Use **patronymics**, caste titles, village names, or initials
* May change or drop surnames across generations

Example patterns:

* *Given name + father’s initial*
* *Caste or honorific as last name*
* *Village name as identifier*
